## 1. Key Decisions & Problem Solutions

* **Partner Collaboration:** The organization collaborates with external bodies to manage and evaluate awards.
* **Anti-Sabotage & Verification:**
    * Submissions require a designated company email.
    * The person submitting must provide official ID (PAN card).
    * System uses a verification pattern similar to GST checks to block fake entries.
* **Duplicate Entry Fix:**
    * System strips common suffixes (`Ltd.`, `Co.`, `Inc.`).
    * Similar names are grouped and reviewed.
    * Verified matches merge into one official profile.
