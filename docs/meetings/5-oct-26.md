Date: 5 October 2026 Purpose: Understand the organisation, its purpose, and how applicants are reached and treated Status: Discovery session completed; points below are confirmed understandings

1. Summary

This first session built a basic understanding of what an organisation is, why it exists, and what it does. It also covered how judges' comfort relates to their relationship with applicants, how applicants learn about the awards, and the principles of fair treatment and ease of use. A reference organisation was recommended for further research, which helped reframe the problem statement.

2. Understanding the Organisation
   Discussed what an organisation is, why it exists, and what it does.
   The goal was to understand the context the awards program operates in before defining requirements.
   2.1 Recommended Research Reference
   CII (Confederation of Indian Industry) was recommended as a reference organisation for research on this topic.
   Studying CII helped rediscover the problem statement in a new way.

Implication: The problem statement should be revisited and reworded in light of this research.

3. Judging Comfort and Relationship with Applicants
   Judges' comfort during judging was discussed.
   This comfort can be related to the relationship between the judge and the applicant.

Implication: The system should consider how familiarity between judges and applicants affects fairness. This links to the conflict-of-interest point in Section 8.

4. How Applicants Reach the Awards

Applicants are reached through three channels:

Email sent to previous years' participants.
Campaigns to promote the awards.
Recommendations from others.

Implication: The system should support bulk email to past participants, campaign links or tracking, and a way to accept recommended applicants.

5. Fair and Equal Treatment
   No priority is given to members of the organisation.
   All applicants are treated equally and without bias.

Implication: Membership status must not influence entry, scoring, or results.

6. Ease of Use for Staff
   Staff must be able to edit the registration form themselves.
   A non-technical person should be able to do this easily, without much trouble.

Implication: The system needs a simple form editor (for example, drag and drop or plain fields) that requires no coding.

7. Definition: "Region"
   A region is defined as a location.
   It is not treated as a subdivision of a national competition.

Implication: Regional entries are independent, location-based entries, not stages that feed into a national award.
