## 1. Applicants

- Applicants can **browse** the application.
- To **register**, they must first **verify** themselves.
  - Organisations are verified by their **GST number**.
  - **Proposed addition:** also ask for the **PAN of the organisation holder**, then let them continue with registration.
  - This helps to **prevent impersonation** and to keep **one report per organisation**.
- After registering, the applicant **fills in the form** with the required details and **submits** it.
- The form is **saved midway**. If the applicant leaves while filling it, their progress is kept, because filling the form takes a lot of time.
- The applicant can see the **status of their report**:
  - In the report pool
  - Assigned
  - Being worked on
  - Graded successfully
- After the results are out, the applicant can see the **result on the leaderboard**.

---

## 2. Judges

- The judge's workflow starts with an **email** that contains the link for the judge's application.
- The link takes the judge **directly to the judging tab(can be changed later)**, where they see the reports **assigned to them**.
- For each report, the judge can **accept or decline**.
  - If the judge **declines**, the report goes back to the **report allocation pool**.
  - If the judge **accepts**, they get access to the report and can **grade** it.
- **Conflict declaration removed:** judges will **not** declare conflicts with applicants. Judges would rarely tell the organisers about a conflict, since it may benefit them if nobody knows.
- **Partial grading is saved.** A judge can grade part of the report and finish later. Judges are experienced people in their fields and very busy, so they may not finish grading in one go.
- After grading is done, the judge can **change** their scores.
  - This applies to **every part** of the report.
  - A change in a score needs a **reason**.
  - To change the score of an already graded report, the judge must **contact staff for permission**. Staff **ask questions and confirm** whether the change should be allowed.

---

## 3. Round 2 (Judges)

- Judges selected for **round 2** connect with the organisation in a **meeting**.
- Usually a **group of judges** is present in the **online meeting**.
- They evaluate the organisation and **grade them for the award**.

---

## 4. Staff

Staff are the **most important** role. Their responsibilities are:

- **Configure the forms** and change them according to recommendations.
- **Select judges** according to the **priority list**.
- **Coordinate events**.
- **Communicate with applicants** to resolve their problems.
- **Communicate with judges** to resolve their issues and give suitable solutions.
- **Confirm applicants**.
- Check for **conflicts between judges and applicants**.
- **Report and document** the process of the events and manage them.
- **Assign reports to judges** according to the requirements.
- **Balance the workload** of judges, so that not too many reports are assigned to a single judge.
- Decide **how many judges** should be assigned to a report, and maintain balance among them.
- **Give judges access to change scores**, according to the condition the judge provides.
- **Give applicants access to change their report** in certain conditions or issues, if possible.

---

## 5. Leadership

- Leadership can see the **dashboard of the whole event**, for example:
  - Reports under evaluation
  - Reports in the report pool
  - Reports rejected or failed the proof test
  - Many other event details

---

## 6. Key Points Decided

1. Organisation verification by GST, with the PAN of the organisation holder proposed as an addition.
2. One report per organisation.
3. Applicant forms are saved midway.
4. Judges accept or decline a report; declined reports return to the pool.
5. Judges do not declare conflicts; staff check for conflicts.
6. Judges' grading is saved midway.
7. Every score change needs a reason.
8. Changing the score of a graded report needs staff permission.
9. Round 2 is an online meeting with a group of judges.
10. Staff control forms, judge selection, report assignment and workload balance.
