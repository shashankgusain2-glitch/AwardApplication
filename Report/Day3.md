Daily Report: One-to-One Meeting Summary

Date: 05-oct-26



1. Purpose

To revisit the project's problem statement, understand the organisation's needs more deeply, and clarify key points on fairness, forms, regions, and communication.

2. Key Discussion Points

1. Re-understanding the problem and the organisation
   The problem statement and the organisation's structure and goals were revisited. This will guide the next steps so the solution matches how the organisation actually works.

2. Fair and unbiased judging
   Members who use the organisation's portal will get no priority or advantage. All applicants must be evaluated on equal terms, and judging must be unbiased.

3. Form management by non-technical users
   The technical team will confirm that a person with no technical background can update the forms easily, without developer support.

4. Definition of "region"
   A region will be defined as a location. It will not be treated as a subdivision of a national competition.

5. Conflict of interest
   A process is needed to identify and avoid conflicts of interest between judges and applicants, so no judge evaluates an applicant they are connected to.

6. Communication
   More frequent and clearer communication is needed between the team and the organisation.
